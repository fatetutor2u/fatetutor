Qi Men Dun Jia: Set the Hour Chart

Qi Men Dun Jia is one of the three classical methods (三式). From the solar term and the day it finds the 局, lays the wonders and instruments on nine palaces, then turns the stars, doors and gods to the hour. This page sets the chart as the Ming and Qing books do, and shows the rule behind each step.

The moment

Which hour?

· The day and the 局 turn at 23:00, the start of the 子 hour. Solar terms are dated on your local calendar. The moment is set to now when the page opens.

How the 局 is found
Enter the 局 and hour myself

For a chart from a book or a teacher: pick 陽 or 陰, the number, and the hour's 干支.

The chart is worked out on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

How the chart is set, in the books' words

Each term has three 局 (上, 中, 下元), five days each:

「冬至惊蛰一七四」

The 元 starts on a 甲 or 己 day (符頭):

「甲己二日号符头，子午卯酉为上列。寅申巳亥配中元，辰戌丑未下元节。」

The 局 may run ahead of the term (超) and is brought back by a repeated term (閏), only at 芒種 or 大雪:

「置闰定在芒种、大雪之后」

Instruments forward and wonders backward in 陽遁, the reverse in 陰遁:

「陽遁順儀奇逆布，陰遁逆儀奇順行。」

The chief star goes onto the hour stem; the envoy door counts the hours:

「九宮逢甲為直符，八門值使自分明。符上之門為直使，十時一易堪憑據。值符常遣加時干，值使順逆遁宮去。」

The eight gods start from the chief star:

「值符前三六合位，太陰之神在前二。後一宮中為九天，後二之神為九地。」

Quoted as printed (Chinese Wikisource), public domain: 煙波釣叟歌, 遁甲演義, 奇門遁甲統宗.

The nine palaces (九宮)

Laid out as the book draws it, south at the top. In each palace: the god, the star with the stem it carries (heaven plate), the door, and the earth-plate stem at the foot.

Middle palace: its stem travels with the star of 坤. 「甲辰五，乃寄在坤二宫」

Reading the chart

The earth plate stays put for the five days of the 元. Each hour the stars turn as one ring onto the hour stem, the doors turn as another ring counted by the hour, and the gods follow the chief star.

Stars, doors and gods are shown by their Chinese names with pinyin. Fate Tutor does not translate or explain them as good or bad.

These are the facts the books read from. Fate Tutor does not turn them into a forecast.

What this page leaves out, and why

The named patterns (格局), the readings of stars, doors and gods, the birth year and running year, and the charms and spells in the books are not shown: they turn the chart into judgements or rituals. Year, month and day charts are not set either; this page sets the hour chart.

The modern split-and-fill method (拆補) is shown only as a switch. The Qing imperial 御定奇門寶鑑 rejects it: 「何得创为拆补以乱符头」

The books disagree on how far the 局 may run ahead before the leap; the page uses nine days counting both ends, as in 統宗's dated example for 1718, and the checker repeats that example.

Where this comes from

煙波釣叟歌, 遁甲演義 and 奇門遁甲統宗

The core verse 煙波釣叟歌 (Song, revised in the Ming) gives the turning of the plate. 遁甲演義 (程道生, Ming, in the 四庫全書) gives the 局 for each term, the leap rule and two worked charts; 奇門遁甲統宗 (Ming) gives the same verse and a dated leap run for 1717 to 1719. All are public domain on Chinese Wikisource; 御定奇門寶鑑 (Qing) was read as a further witness.

Checked against the books: the engine gives both worked charts of 遁甲演義 and 統宗's dated leap of 1718, and all 1,080 plates agree with a second version built from the books' tables. The online copies have a few slips, such as 八五二 for 八二五 at 小暑; our checker names each one.

About this tradition

One of the three classical methods

Qi Men Dun Jia, with 大六壬 and 太乙, makes up the three classical methods (三式). The hour chart is built only from the solar term, the day and the hour.

Fate Tutor sets the chart and shows its sources. It does not turn a chart into a forecast, financial advice, medical advice or a decision made for you.

These are traditional systems of belief. They are not science and they do not predict the future. Fate Tutor shows what each tradition says and where it says it, in the language of tendencies.
