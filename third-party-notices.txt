Fate Tutor — open-source notices
================================

The calculators on www.fatetutor.com are written in Rust and run in your browser.
They include the open-source libraries listed below. Each library remains the
work of its authors and is used under the licence shown.

Data and reference works
------------------------
- City names, coordinates and time zones: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org),
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The list was trimmed and reformatted.
- Time-zone history: the IANA Time Zone Database (public domain), as packaged by
  the jiff-tzdb library below.
- Position of the Sun: the VSOP87 theory of P. Bretagnon and G. Francou (1988),
  as implemented by the vsop87 library below.
- Position of the Moon and other formulas: Jean Meeus, "Astronomical Algorithms".
- Classical Chinese texts are in the public domain and were read from Chinese
  Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org).
- Fonts (Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk) are loaded from
  Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License; they are not part of this site's files.

The Rust standard library is also built in. It is dual-licensed MIT / Apache-2.0,
copyright The Rust Project Contributors (https://www.rust-lang.org).
