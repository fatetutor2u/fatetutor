Zi Wei Dou Shu: Set Out Your Chart

Zi Wei Dou Shu places stars in twelve palaces from your lunar birth date and hour. This page sets out the chart exactly as the Ming handbook 紫微斗數全書 does and shows every step. It does not read the chart for you.

How the chart is set, in the book's words

命 and 身 from the birth month and hour:

「大抵人命俱从寅上起正月，顺数至本生月止，又自人生月起子时逆至本生时安命，顺至本生时安身。」

The twelve palaces run backward:

「男女俱从逆转切忌莫顺去」

「甲己之岁起丙寅，乙庚之岁起戊寅，丙辛之岁起庚寅，丁壬之岁起壬寅，戊癸之岁起甲寅。」

「如是正月初一生者是火局，酉宫起初一日，就从酉宫起紫微」

Quoted from 紫微斗數全書 卷二 as printed, public domain.

How your chart was set

    Reading the chart

    Each square is a palace, named by its branch (子 … 亥) and stem. 命 marks the Life palace and 身 the Body palace. Bold names are the fourteen major stars; the others are helper stars.

    The fourteen major stars follow 紫微 and 天府:

    「紫微天机逆行旁，隔一阳武天同当，又隔二位廉贞地，空三复见紫微郎，」

    「天府太阴与贪狼，巨门天相及天梁，七杀空三破军位，八星顺数细推详。」

    「天府惟寅申二宫紫府同宫」

    祿, 權, 科 and 忌 are the four transformations (四化), set by the birth year's stem:

    「如甲生人廉贞化禄、破军化权、武曲化科、太阳化忌是也」

    魁鉞: the book's verse names the two palaces of this pair without saying which is 天魁 and which is 天鉞, so both are marked 魁鉞.

    Not shown, and why

    火星 and 鈴星: the verse gives only the palaces they start from, not which way to count, so they are left out: 「寅午戌人丑卯方」

    大限 (ten-year periods): this book starts them one palace away from 命, while the Daoist-canon text 紫微斗數 starts them at 命. They are not shown until that is settled.

    What the stars mean: this page sets out the chart only. Fate Tutor does not turn it into a forecast.

    Where this comes from

    紫微斗數全書, volume two

    Every rule comes from 紫微斗數全書 (a Ming text, Qing edition), volume two, on Chinese Wikisource, which is public domain. Quotes are given as the online copy prints them.

    Checked cell by cell: the engine places 紫微 exactly as the book's five drawn tables do for all 150 days. Two slips in the online copy are named in our checker: the wood table prints 初九 twice, and the metal table leaves out day 30.

    About this tradition

    A chart of twelve palaces

    Zi Wei Dou Shu, the 'Purple Star' method, is traditionally linked with the Song-era Daoist 陳摶. It turns the lunar birth date and hour into a ring of twelve palaces, each holding stars.

    Fate Tutor sets out the chart and shows its sources. It does not turn a chart into a forecast, financial advice, medical advice or a decision made for you.

    These are traditional systems of belief. They are not science and they do not predict the future. Fate Tutor shows what each tradition says and where it says it, in the language of tendencies.
