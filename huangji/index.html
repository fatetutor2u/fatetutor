Huangji Jingshi: Where a Year Stands in Shao Yong's Cycle

The Song philosopher 邵雍 (1011–1077) set all of history inside one great cycle of 129,600 years. This page places any year in that cycle, counted exactly as the tables of his 皇極經世書, and shows the book's own entry for the years it records. It is a count, not a forecast.

The count, in the book's words

The four levels are named after the sun, moon, stars and zodiac hours:

「日經天之元月經天之㑹星經天之運辰經天之世」

One 元 holds 12 會, 360 運 and 4,320 世:

「元之元一元之㑹十二元之運三百六十元之世四千三百二十」

Each 世 is thirty years:

「古者謂三十年為一世」

So one 元 is 129,600 years:

「天之運行有一十二萬九千六百之年」

Things begin in the middle of the 寅 會 and close in the 戌 會:

「開物始月寅之中」

「閉物經月戌之終」

The book's chronicle runs from Emperor Yao's accession to the Later Zhou:

「起於帝堯甲辰至後周己未」

「甲辰唐帝堯肇位于平陽」

Quoted as printed: 皇極經世書, 四庫全書 edition, public domain on Chinese Wikisource.

How it was counted

    Reading the cycle

    Labels follow the book: 會 by the twelve branches from 子, 運 by the ten stems from 甲, and 世 again by the branches. So 會 午 7 is the seventh 會, and 運 乙 192 the 192nd 運.

    For 2367 BC to 963 AD each year is checked against the book's own list: every 世 heading and every year's 干支 agree with the count.

    This is where the book's count puts the year. Fate Tutor does not turn it into a forecast.

    What this page leaves out, and why

    Hexagrams for each 會, 運 and 世: they are often quoted, but this book's tables do not give them. They come from later commentators (張行成, 祝泌), so they are not shown.

    The sound and rhyme tables (聲音唱和, volumes 7 to 10): they concern the sounds of words, not the count of years.

    The Qing editors printed their own doubt in the book's preface, saying the division into 元, 會, 運 and 世 has nothing to rest on: 「元㑹運世之分無所依據」

    The online copy is missing 415 to 464 AD, so no entry is shown for those years. Its volumes 1 and 2 run some table columns together, so the check rests on volumes 3 to 6.

    Where this comes from

    皇極經世書, 四庫全書 edition

    邵雍's 皇極經世書 in the 四庫全書 edition (1781), all fourteen volumes, public domain on Chinese Wikisource. The count comes from volumes 3 to 6 and 12; the chronicle from volumes 5 and 6.

    Checked: all 240 運 of the 以會經運 tables and all 3,280 years of the chronicle agree with the count. A few characters the online copy prints wrongly (己 for 巳, 戌 for 戊 and the like) are named in our checker.

    About this tradition

    History inside a cycle

    邵雍 (1011–1077) was a Song-dynasty philosopher in a line of teaching that runs back to 陳摶. 皇極經世書 sets out his numbers: one cycle for heaven and earth, with the course of history placed inside it.

    Fate Tutor sets out the count and shows its sources. It does not turn it into a forecast, financial advice, medical advice or a decision made for you.

    These are traditional systems of belief. They are not science and they do not predict the future. Fate Tutor shows what each tradition says and where it says it, in the language of tendencies.
